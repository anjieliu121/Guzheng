ARGUMENT & REASONING 
• Clarity of author’s claim 
• Originality and significance of contribution to the field 
• Logical development 
• Coherence 
• Strength of overall analysis 
• Methodological approach 
• Acknowledgement and handling of other perspectives/approaches/claims 
• Organization and structure

EVIDENCE & ENGAGEMENT WITH LITERATURE 
• Demonstrated familiarity with key experts/issues/texts in the field 
• Appropriate, critical engagement with scholarly sources 
• Grounding of claim in appropriate, sufficient, and timely evidence 
• Accurate and appropriate analysis of data/evidence 
• Clarity of visual evidence, if any 
• Effective integration of quotes, if applicable

WRITTEN PRESENTATION 
• Overall readability 
• Accessibility to a non-specialist audience 
• Appropriate/scholarly tone 
• Flow and transitions 
• Grammar and mechanics 
• Appropriate document formatting 
• Appropriate headings/subheadings 
• APA citation/References formatting
